Privacy Policy

Updated November 10, 2026

Vacation Condo Oahu (vacationcondooahu.com) presents advertising partnerships for owners and managers of short-term rentals on Oʻahu. This page explains what information the site collects.

Partnership inquiries

If you contact us through the form, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, contact details, property information and message. It is delivered via the lead server of Eye To Ad Media (clients.eyetoad.com), which runs this site, and used only to respond to you about advertising. We don't sell or share it with other companies.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 uses cookies to report on visits: pages viewed, device, approximate region and referral source. We look at these figures in aggregate.

Google AdSense cookies

This site may show ads through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

Opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at www.aboutads.info. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your choices

Browsers let you block or delete cookies. To ask what we hold from an inquiry or have it removed, email (shown when the page loads) or call 1-800-481-8638.

Children and changes

Not intended for children under 13. Changes to this policy update the date above.
